BettorIndex is a sports betting research tool for **NBA player props** and **NBA game lines** — moneyline, spread, and total markets on one research card per matchup.

## What can you research for NBA?

**Prop Markets** — player Over/Under props with hit rates, home/away clears, relevant injuries, and Market Read (Opportunity, Worth Watching, Potential Pass).

**Game Lines** — team moneyline, spread, and total research with venue and head-to-head records, trend statuses (Trend Favors, Trend Supports, Mixed Signals, or No Clear Trend), best odds, split-market ranges, and injuries for both teams.

## How is NBA prop research different from game lines?

Props use weighted hit-rate trends plus implied-odds comparison. Game lines use venue + head-to-head rates (covers for spreads, clear rates for totals). Last-N overall records on game lines are context only.

## Which sportsbooks does BettorIndex track for NBA?

BetMGM, DraftKings, FanDuel, and Fanatics. Each card surfaces the best available line and price for the leaned side or direction — not a full odds board.
