BettorIndex researches team **moneyline**, **spread**, and **total** markets with one card structure across NBA, MLB, and NFL. Each card shows a lean, the records that drive it, best line/odds, and relevant injuries for both teams.

## How does moneyline research work?

Moneyline leans combine each team's **venue win rate** and **head-to-head win rate** when enough matchup history exists (at least three head-to-head games). Cards show plain-language records like "Won 6 of last 10 away games."

**Last-N overall wins are shown for context only** — they do not feed the lean. When books disagree on price, the card shows a **Split Market** price range per team.

## How does spread research work?

Spread research uses the same structure as moneyline, but with **cover rates** instead of wins. Each team's score is venue cover rate plus head-to-head cover rate.

Cards show "Covered X of last Y games" language and surface the **best spread line and price**. When books post different spread lines, the card shows line and price ranges — informational only.

## How does total research work?

Totals are **not team-vs-team picks**. BettorIndex researches whether recent game history favors the **Over** or **Under** at tonight's total.

Each direction's score combines:

- Home-team clear rate
- Away-team clear rate
- Head-to-head clear rate (when enough sample exists)

The lean is **Over** or **Under**, not a team name. Best line shopping uses the minimum total for Over and maximum total for Under.

## What is the opposing side section?

For moneyline and spread cards, expand the **opposing team** section to compare both sides. For totals, expand the **other direction** (Over vs Under) instead.

## What about injuries on game lines?

Game line cards show **relevant injuries for both teams** in the matchup — not a full league injury report. Same idea as props, scoped to tonight's game.
