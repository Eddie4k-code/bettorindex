BettorIndex researches team **moneyline**, **spread**, and **total** markets with one card structure across NBA, MLB, and NFL. Each card shows a **trend status**, the records behind it, best line/odds, and relevant injuries for both teams.

## Game line trend statuses

Every game line card classifies into one of four trend statuses:

- **Trend Favors {Outcome}** — venue and head-to-head signals clearly agree and are well above a coin flip (for example, strong home/away records plus a solid head-to-head split).
- **Trend Supports {Outcome}** — venue and head-to-head trends point the same way, but recent records are not strong enough for a clear favor.
- **Mixed Signals** — venue or team-context trends and head-to-head history disagree. The card does not pick a side; it explains that recent trends do not clearly favor either outcome.
- **No Clear Trend** — recent records are too close to even to favor either side or direction, or scoring ties between both outcomes.

When **Mixed Signals** or **No Clear Trend** applies, cards show a neutral sentence: recent team trends do not clearly favor either outcome.

## How does moneyline research work?

Moneyline cards compare each team's **venue win rate** and **head-to-head win rate** when enough matchup history exists (at least three head-to-head games). Cards show plain-language records like "Won 6 of last 10 away games."

**Last-N overall wins are shown for context only** — they do not drive the trend. When books disagree on price, the card shows a **Split Market** price range per team.

## How does spread research work?

Spread research uses the same structure as moneyline, but with **cover rates** instead of wins. Each direction's score combines venue cover rate plus head-to-head cover rate.

Cards show "Covered X of last Y games" language and surface the **best spread line and price**. When books post different spread lines, the card shows line and price ranges — informational only.

## How does total research work?

Totals are **not team-vs-team picks**. BettorIndex researches whether recent game history favors the **Over** or **Under** at tonight's total.

Each direction's score combines:

- Home-team clear rate
- Away-team clear rate
- Head-to-head clear rate (when enough sample exists)

The trend status names **Over** or **Under** when signals agree — not a team name. Best line shopping uses the minimum total for Over and maximum total for Under.

## What is the opposing side section?

When **Trend Favors** applies, the favored team or direction is shown up front with best line/odds. For moneyline and spread cards, expand the **opposing team** section to compare both sides. For totals, expand the **other direction** (Over vs Under) instead.

## What about injuries on game lines?

Game line cards show **relevant injuries for both teams** in the matchup — not a full league injury report. Same idea as props, scoped to tonight's game.
